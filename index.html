Quick Answer  |  What OREXITY Does?

How OREXITY Accelerates Mineral Investment Decisions?

  • Consultancy: opportunity screening, review of available technical information, project maturity assessment, risk identification, and development planning.
  • Investment Advisory: commercial analysis, financial scenarios, investment readiness, transaction structuring, and partner alignment.
  • Projects: selected opportunities presented through a defined evidence base, project status, material risks, next milestones, and potential investment pathways.
  • Technical frameworks: where appropriate to the project and scope, work may be considered against relevant reporting and valuation frameworks, including JORC, NI 43-101, and VALMIN. Such references do not imply certification or compliance unless independently established.

Investment Advisory

Capital Discipline Before Capital Commitment.

OREXITY delivers structured mineral investment advisory and project assessment services to investors, mineral-rights holders and strategic partners evaluating opportunities in Pakistan.

01

Opportunity Screening and Due Diligence

  • Initial review of the asset, ownership and available project information
  • Coordination of legal, tenure, technical, commercial and ESG due diligence
  • Identification of material risks, assumptions and unresolved questions
  • Assessment of project maturity and the evidence required for the next decision
  • Structured decision briefs for investment committees and potential partners
02

Investment Structuring

  • Joint-venture, earn-in and farm-in framework advisory
  • Equity participation and staged capital-deployment options
  • Work-program-linked funding and milestone design
  • Governance, reporting and information-rights considerations
  • Exit-pathway analysis, including strategic sale or other negotiated structures where appropriate
03

Financial and Commercial Analysis

  • Project-level financial modelling and scenario analysis
  • Capital expenditure and operating-cost assumptions review
  • Sensitivity analysis and key-driver assessment
  • Royalty, offtake and other commercial structure considerations
  • Coordination of independent asset valuation where warranted
04

Investor Readiness and Engagement

  • Investment teaser, information memorandum and project presentation support
  • Investor data-room structure and document-index preparation
  • Strategic investor and partner mapping
  • Meeting preparation and cross-border communication coordination
  • Transaction process tracking and follow-up support

A Disciplined Investment Sequence

1

Understand the asset and the proposed transaction.

2

Validate title, data quality, project history and key assumptions through appropriate specialists.

3

Define the next work program, its budget, milestones and decision criteria.

4

Structure investment terms and governance to match the evidence and risk profile.

5

Advance only when the agreed technical, legal, commercial and stakeholder conditions are addressed.

Frequently asked questions

What should investors know?

What does OREXITY do?

OREXITY provides mineral investment advisory and project assessment support, including opportunity screening, due diligence coordination, investment structuring, financial and commercial analysis, investor readiness and engagement.

What technical services are described?

The source describes geological exploration and targeting, geochemical and geophysical surveys, drilling and sampling programme support, technical intelligence and modelling, exploration data management and QA/QC, environmental baseline and field HSE, and technical integration and reporting.

Where is the projects portfolio focused?

The source identifies mineral opportunities associated with Gilgit-Baltistan, Pakistan, and names Markhor 1, Markhor 2, Rakaposhi 1 and Ketu 1.

Does OREXITY guarantee mineral discovery or investment returns?

No. The source expressly states that OREXITY does not guarantee mineral discovery, resource classification, regulatory approvals, investment returns or transaction completion.

When do service scope, deliverables and fees become binding?

The source states that scope, deliverables, fees, payment terms, responsibilities, rights, obligations and other conditions are defined in the relevant written engagement or other duly executed contractual instrument, as applicable.

What are the three key investment priority decisions in OREXITY's process?

Investment Focus (commodities and jurisdictions aligned to the investment mandate), Project Stage & Capital (project stages and investment range), and Investment Criteria (evidence or milestones required before technical review or investment discussions).
