Investment Advisory

Capital Discipline Before Capital Commitment.

OREXITY delivers structured mineral investment advisory and project assessment services to investors, mineral-rights holders and strategic partners evaluating opportunities in Pakistan.

01

Opportunity Screening and Due Diligence

  • Initial review of the asset, ownership and available project information
  • Coordination of legal, tenure, technical, commercial and ESG due diligence
  • Identification of material risks, assumptions and unresolved questions
  • Assessment of project maturity and the evidence required for the next decision
  • Structured decision briefs for investment committees and potential partners
02

Investment Structuring

  • Joint-venture, earn-in and farm-in framework advisory
  • Equity participation and staged capital-deployment options
  • Work-program-linked funding and milestone design
  • Governance, reporting and information-rights considerations
  • Exit-pathway analysis, including strategic sale or other negotiated structures where appropriate
03

Financial and Commercial Analysis

  • Project-level financial modelling and scenario analysis
  • Capital expenditure and operating-cost assumptions review
  • Sensitivity analysis and key-driver assessment
  • Royalty, offtake and other commercial structure considerations
  • Coordination of independent asset valuation where warranted
04

Investor Readiness and Engagement

  • Investment teaser, information memorandum and project presentation support
  • Investor data-room structure and document-index preparation
  • Strategic investor and partner mapping
  • Meeting preparation and cross-border communication coordination
  • Transaction process tracking and follow-up support

A Disciplined Investment Sequence

1

Understand the asset and the proposed transaction.

2

Validate title, data quality, project history and key assumptions through appropriate specialists.

3

Define the next work program, its budget, milestones and decision criteria.

4

Structure investment terms and governance to match the evidence and risk profile.

5

Advance only when the agreed technical, legal, commercial and stakeholder conditions are addressed.
