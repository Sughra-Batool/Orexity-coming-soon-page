PRIVACY POLICY

OREXITY | Mineral Investment & Advisory Platform

Last Updated: November 2026

1. INTRODUCTION

OREXITY ("OREXITY", "we", "our" or "us") respects the privacy and confidentiality of individuals, investors, companies and business partners engaging with our platform.

Privacy Policy governs the collection, use, processing, storage and disclosure of information obtained through www.orexity.com, online forms, business enquiries and professional engagements.

We are committed to responsible information management, commercial confidentiality and appropriate data protection in accordance with applicable laws.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Depending on the nature of your engagement, OREXITY may collect:

  • Personal Information: Name, designation, email address, telephone number and professional details.
  • Corporate Information: Company profile, ownership structure, business interests and organisational details.
  • Investment Information: Investment capacity, capital range, commodity preferences, geographical interests and partnership objectives.
  • Technical and Project Information: Geological reports, exploration data, licences, maps, assay results, technical assessments and project documentation.
  • Technical Website Information: IP address, browser type, device information, website activity and cookie-related data.

3. PURPOSE OF INFORMATION PROCESSING

Information is collected and processed for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Responding to enquiries and managing professional communications.
  • Assessing investor interests and potential business alignment.
  • Evaluating mineral projects and investment opportunities.
  • Preparing proposals, project profiles and advisory documentation.
  • Supporting technical and commercial due diligence.
  • Coordinating engagements with professional advisers and strategic partners.
  • Managing contractual, administrative and legal obligations.
  • Maintaining website security and improving user experience.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY AND INFORMATION PROTECTION

OREXITY recognises the commercial sensitivity of investor identities, investment intentions, corporate information, technical reports and non-public mineral project documentation.

Such information is handled on a need-to-know basis and used for relevant business purposes.

Where appropriate, additional confidentiality arrangements, including Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) and contractual safeguards, may be established before the exchange of sensitive information.

Submission of information through the website does not, by itself, establish a contractual NDA, exclusive engagement, fiduciary relationship or investment commitment.

5. INFORMATION SHARING AND DISCLOSURE

OREXITY does not sell personal information or investor contact details to third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

Information may be shared, where reasonably necessary, with:

  • Authorised professional advisers, legal consultants and technical experts.
  • Prospective investors and strategic partners for relevant project engagements.
  • Relevant government departments and public institutions for legitimate coordination or compliance purposes.
  • Website hosting, cloud storage, cybersecurity and technology service providers.
  • Competent authorities where disclosure is required by applicable law.

Information sharing is limited to the relevant business or legal purpose and subject to appropriate confidentiality safeguards.

6. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

OREXITY engages with international investors, companies and professional partners. Accordingly, information may be processed or accessed outside Pakistan.

Where applicable, reasonable contractual, technical and organisational safeguards will be applied to international information transfers in accordance with relevant data protection requirements.

7. DATA SECURITY

OREXITY applies reasonable administrative, technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss or misuse.

These measures may include access restrictions, password protection, secure communication channels and controlled document permissions.

However, no electronic system or internet-based communication can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. COOKIES AND WEBSITE TECHNOLOGIES

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to maintain functionality, improve performance, analyse website traffic and enhance user experience.

Users may manage cookie preferences through available website controls or browser settings. Certain website functions may be affected if cookies are disabled.

Third-party services may operate under their own privacy policies.

9. DATA RETENTION

OREXITY retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary for its intended business purposes, contractual obligations, legal compliance, record-keeping and legitimate business interests.

Information that is no longer required may be securely deleted, anonymised or otherwise disposed of, subject to applicable legal requirements.

10. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

Subject to applicable law, individuals may request:

  • Access to their personal information.
  • Correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion of information where legally permissible.
  • Withdrawal of consent where processing is consent-based.
  • Restriction or objection to certain processing activities where applicable.
  • Cessation of non-essential marketing communications.

Requests may be submitted through our official contact details. Identity verification may be required before processing such requests.

11. THIRD-PARTY WEBSITES

Our website may contain links to external websites, institutions and service providers. OREXITY does not control their privacy practices, content or security arrangements.

Users are advised to review the applicable privacy policies before sharing information with third-party platforms.

12. POLICY UPDATES

OREXITY may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in applicable legislation, business operations, technology or information management practices.

Revised versions will be published on www.orexity.com with an updated revision date.

13. APPLICABLE LAW

This Policy shall be interpreted in accordance with applicable laws of Pakistan. Where relevant, mandatory foreign data protection requirements, including the GDPR, may also apply to qualifying processing activities.

Nothing in this Policy limits rights or obligations that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable legislation.
